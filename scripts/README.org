#+TITLE: scripts/ -- Data pipeline
#+OPTIONS: toc:1

Babashka namespaces loaded through the =:paths= of =bb.edn=: =common=
(I/O, decision files, country tables), =enrich= (enrichment sources),
=registries= (the gov.uk registry), =indegree= (link graph), =probes=
(the =data/.db= state) and =pipeline= (harvest, scoring, policy table,
dispatcher). =detect-from-directories.clj= (=bb directories=) harvests
the directories of public bodies whose entry in =sources_domains.yaml=
of swh-sopc-data-sources carries a =harvest:= spec (its keys are
documented at the top of the script). The other sources read their
URL there too, through the =id:= of their entry (=common/sopc-data-url=):
=cddo= (=govuk=) and =linkgraph= (=indegree=).

* Decision files and generated files

The confirmed domains of a country are compiled in memory at each run
(=common/compile-confirmed=): its registries
(=sources/<registry>/registered.csv=, =domain,level=, written by
=govuk= and =bb directories registry=) plus =curated.csv=
(=domain,level,kind=, a curated row winning) minus =excluded.csv=
(=domain,reason=). A contradiction stops every command; =bb check=
reports them all, with their fix. A =local= row or an excluded label
directly under a central root takes that label out of the root's
subtree; it is never harvested.

Generated per country: =proposed.csv=, =to_curate.csv= (=proposed.csv=
minus what was decided since, in the columns of =curated.csv=, a domain
standing for its subdomains, two or more under one registrable domain
folded into it; =bb todo= refreshes it offline),
=summary.md= and =sources/=.

The harvest has one file per harvested root,
=sources/harvest/<root>.csv= (one column =subdomain=, apex included):
=fetch <root>= creates it for a confirmed =central= or =central-1=
domain; other confirmed roots only get their apex probed. A host under
two harvested roots stays in the deeper file (=normalize=). =wayback
<root>…= adds the hosts the Wayback Machine captured, on demand (~30 s
a page).

The HTTPS status and MX of each host and the outcome of each crt.sh
fetch, all dated, live in the local [[https://github.com/datalevin/datalevin][Datalevin]] database =data/.db=
(git-ignored). Probes run on unprobed hosts, and with =MAX_AGE=<days>=
on older ones; =retry= replays the failed fetches. =aggregate= publishes
the last HTTPS status and MX of every host in
=data/public-sector-domains.csv=, which other repositories read rather
than the database (swh-sopc-data-sources, to leave the closed hosts out
of its forge scan).

* Commands

=bb pipeline <cmd>= or =bb scripts/pipeline.clj <cmd>=; four phases
chain the targeted commands:

#+begin_src bash
  bb pipeline collect    # fetch (+ retry), normalize, probe, mx, probe-roots
  bb pipeline enrich     # build-qid, build-gec, build-un-ids, subdivisions, then the sources
  bb pipeline build      # aggregate, domains (after enrich)
  bb pipeline report     # propose (+ to_curate.csv), summary (after build)
  bb pipeline all        # the four in sequence
#+end_src

#+begin_src bash
  # collect -- sources/harvest/ and data/.db
  fetch [DOM…]            # crt.sh (MAX_AGE: only the roots due)
  retry [DOM…]            # the roots whose last fetch failed
  normalize               # clean the harvest files
  probe [DOM…]            # HTTPS HEAD of the harvested hosts
  mx [DOM…]               # DNS MX of the harvested hosts
  probe-roots [C…]        # HTTPS + MX of the confirmed roots without a harvest

  # enrich -- sources/ and data/sources/
  build-qid | build-gec | build-un-ids   # country_dir -> source id files
  subdivisions [Q:C…]     # first-level subdivisions (Wikidata P150)
  wikidata [Q:C…]         # central administration -> sources/wikidata/central_admin.csv
  academia [Q:C…]         # universities and research institutes -> sources/wikidata/academia.csv
  ror [C…]                # public universities per ROR -> sources/ror/universities.csv
  sopc [C…]               # hosts of the code sources of swh-sopc-data-sources
  iana | cia | un-desa [C…]  # one row per country in data/sources/

  # build -- data/
  aggregate               # data/public-sector-domains.csv (hosts, HTTPS status, MX)
  domains                 # data/public-sector-domains-central+-policy.csv

  # report -- countries/<c>/
  propose [C…]            # proposed.csv, to_curate.csv
  todo [C…]               # to_curate.csv, offline
  summary [C…]            # summary.md
  probe-proposed [C…]     # HTTPS + MX of the proposed hosts

  # other
  wayback DOM…            # Wayback Machine CDX harvest, on demand
  indegree [C…]           # link-graph in-degree -> sources/linkgraph/indegree.csv
  check [C…]              # the decision files (also bb check FRA)
  govuk                   # the UK registry (CDDO list)
#+end_src

=C= is a country dir or its ISO3 code (=FRA_france=, =FRA=), =Q:C= a
country dir or a =QID:country_dir= pair. An enrichment file already
there is skipped unless =FORCE=1=; a failed query leaves it untouched.

* The policy table

=domains= writes =data/public-sector-domains-central+-policy.csv=
(=domain,kind,country=) from the confirmed =central= and =central-1=
domains of UN members and observers: =subtree= (a domain and
everything below it), =exact= (the domain alone, when =curated.csv=
says so) and =exclude= (a =local= or excluded label directly under a
central root). The longest matching domain decides; a domain a subtree
covers is pruned. It is read by [[https://gitlab.softwareheritage.org/swh/products/insights/swh-institutional-analysis][swh-institutional-analysis]].

A root falls under one of three regimes:

1. *One level under the root* (=gouv.fr=, =gc.ca=, =go.jp=): a single
   =subtree= row.
2. *Lower tiers under known labels* (=gov.br=, =gov.au=, =gov.uk=):
   the first-tier bodies (=sp.gov.br=) are =central-1= rows, covered by
   the root with everything below them, unless their root also carries
   a lower tier: the 27 Brazilian states, which host their
   municipalities, are =exact=. Lower-tier labels are =local= rows,
   hence =exclude= rows. For =gov.uk=, =bb pipeline govuk= classifies
   the official CDDO list (Wikidata GSS codes and naming conventions)
   into =sources/govuk/registered.csv=; refresh it after updating the
   =data:= URL of the id =cddo= in =sources_domains.yaml=.
3. *Levels not separable by shape* (US =.gov=): the central domains come
   from an authoritative register (CISA, =bb directories registry
   USA=).

* Scoring (propose)

For each candidate no confirmed or excluded domain covers:

| Criterion                                                        | Points          |
|------------------------------------------------------------------+-----------------|
| UN/DESA-declared national portal                                 | +5              |
| Per distinct Wikidata mention (2 at most)                        | +3              |
| Under the country's primary ccTLD                                | +1              |
| Central pattern (=gov.=, =bund.=, =gouv.=…)                      | +1              |
| Wikidata label naming a Factbook institution                     | +2              |
| In an official directory (=bb directories candidates=)           | +4              |
| A university per ROR                                             | +2              |
| A code-source host of swh-sopc-data-sources                      | +3              |
| Linked from N same-country public-sector domains: N >= 3, 5, 10, 20 | +3, +4, +5, +6 |
| Label naming a territorial subdivision                           | -5              |
| 3+ Wikidata mentions, none federal/national/sovereign            | -5              |

Clamped to =[0, 10]=; the penalties spare hosts known as subnational. A
registrable domain holding three candidates or more gets its own row,
source =parent=. =level= comes from the Wikidata jurisdiction (P1001:
the country, a first-level subdivision or lower), else from the label;
lower-tier hosts are left out.

=academia= and =ror= level universities and research institutes from
their jurisdiction or authorities, else their label (/national/ aside
in the United States), else how the country runs its universities
(=academia-tiers= in =enrich.clj=). =ror= asks Wikidata, through the
QID of each organisation, whether it is private (a private class, or
私立/民办/사립 in its description) or national (国立/國立/국립), and
leaves the private ones out.

* Prerequisites and environment

Babashka 1.12+, and in =data/=: =world-governments.csv= (the countries),
the source id mappings and their aliases, and =public_suffix_list.dat=
(refresh it from https://publicsuffix.org/list/public_suffix_list.dat).

| Var        | Effect                                                                     | Default                    |
|------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------|
| =FORCE=    | overwrite outputs, re-probe every host                                     | unset                      |
| =MAX_AGE=  | days: re-probe older probes, fetch only the roots due                      | unset                      |
| =PARALLEL= | concurrent requests                                                        | varies                     |
| =TIMEOUT=  | HTTPS timeout of a probe, in seconds                                       | =5=                        |
| =SOPC_DIR= | swh-sopc-data-sources checkout: its sources, read by most commands          | =../swh-sopc-data-sources= |
| =CONC_*=   | countries in parallel per source (WIKIDATA, ROR, IANA, CIA, UN_DESA)       | 3, 2, 4, 8, 4              |
