#+title: World Gov domain names

The public-sector domain names of public entities, to measure how much
the public sector contributes to the software archived by [[https://www.softwareheritage.org][Software
Heritage]].

* Levels

- =central= -- the central government: ministries, national agencies and
  research bodies, universities the State runs, and their root domains
  (=gouv.fr=, =gov.uk=).
- =central-1= -- the first tier below (state, Land, region…: =sp.gov.br=,
  =nouvelle-aquitaine.fr=) and the bodies it runs.
- =local= -- anything lower (municipalities, counties…): out of the
  central+ scope, listed only to take a label out of a shared root
  (=abingdon.gov.uk= under =gov.uk=).

* Files

- =data/public-sector-domains-central+-policy.csv= -- the central+ scope
  as a match table, =domain,kind,country=: =subtree= is a domain and
  everything below it, =exact= the domain alone, =exclude= a domain and
  everything below it kept out. The longest matching domain decides.
- =data/public-sector-domains.csv= -- every host found under the
  confirmed domains, with UN status, region, languages and GDP.
- =countries/<ISO3>_<slug>/=, per country:
  - =curated.csv= (=domain,level,name,kind=) and =excluded.csv=
    (=domain,reason=) -- the decisions, edited by hand;
  - =to_curate.csv= -- the proposed domains no one has decided on
    yet, in the columns of =curated.csv=;
  - =proposed.csv= -- the same, scored 0 to 10 with their sources;
  - =summary.md= -- a cross-check report;
  - =sources/= -- the raw data, generated.

The confirmed domains of a country are those of its authoritative
registries (=sources/<registry>/registered.csv=), plus =curated.csv=,
minus =excluded.csv=.

* Sources

Official registries and directories of public bodies (CISA's =.gov=
list, France's =lannuaire= and =esr=, the UK's CDDO list, Norway's
Enhetsregisteret…), Wikidata, [[https://ror.org][ROR]], the link graph of
[[https://github.com/mgifford/eu-plus-government-scans][eu-plus-government-scans]], crt.sh and the Wayback Machine for
subdomains, HTTPS and MX probes (kept in a local [[https://github.com/datalevin/datalevin][Datalevin]] database,
=data/.db=), and country metadata from IANA, UN/DESA, the CIA World
Factbook and the OECD. [[https://gitlab.softwareheritage.org/swh/products/insights/swh-sopc-data-sources][swh-sopc-data-sources]] lists these sources and
looks for government forges and source-code catalogs.

* Running

With [[https://babashka.org][Babashka]]: =bb tasks= lists the tasks, =bb pipeline help= the
commands, =bb pipeline all= runs everything. See =scripts/README.org=.
