#+title: World Gov domain names

This repository collects the government domain names of 197 States and
entities, so we can measure how much governments contribute to
publicly available software archived by Software Heritage.

* Coverage

197 jurisdictions, each tagged with its UN status:

- *193 UN member states*
- *2 UN observer states*: Holy See, Palestine
- *2 non-UN entities*: Taiwan, Kosovo (kept for context, but excluded
  from any UN-facing aggregation).

* Scope

We sort government domains into levels:

- =central= -- the central government: ministries, national agencies
  (including national research bodies: CNRS, CNR…) and the root
  domains that stand for them (=gouv.fr=, =gov.uk=).
- =central-1= -- the first administrative tier below: state, Land,
  region… (=sp.gov.br=, =nouvelle-aquitaine.fr=).
- the rest -- everything lower (municipalities, counties, parishes…),
  kept *out* of the lists.

Universities are out of scope: the strictly public ones (Wikidata
public / public-research / national university classes) are kept as a
parked dataset in =data/public-universities.csv=, refreshed by
=scripts/detect-universities.clj= (=bb universities=), for a possible
later re-exploration. Nothing in the pipeline reads that file.

* Method

We proceed by lists of domain names and subdomains, one country at a
time. The situation varies from country to country: where each level
has its own roots, the lists are enough; but where everything under a
single root like =gov.br= would also include local levels, the local
labels are listed with =level= =local= so that they leave the root.

Per country, two hand-edited files carry every decision:
=curated.csv= (the confirmed domains, with their level) and
=excluded.csv= (what is out of scope). Everything else is generated.
The confirmed domains of a country are the official registries plus
=curated.csv= minus =excluded.csv=, compiled in memory at every run:
nothing to regenerate after a curation.

* What you get

** Consolidated lists

Two ready-to-use CSV files, covering UN members and observers only:

- =data/public-sector-domains.csv= -- every government host we found,
  apex domains *and* subdomains (e.g. =gouv.fr=, =beta.gouv.fr=), with
  its HTTPS status and MX. Each row carries metadata: UN status,
  region, languages and GDP.
- =data/public-sector-domains-central+-policy.csv= -- the central
  government plus the first administrative tier below it (Land, state,
  region…) as a match policy table, =domain,kind,country,level=:
  =subtree= is a domain and everything below it (=gouv.fr=), =exact= a
  domain alone (=sp.gov.br=), =exclude= a domain and everything below
  it to keep out (=abingdon.gov.uk=); =level= is the tier of the
  domain, =central=, =central-1= or =local=. When a host falls under
  several rows, the most specific one (the longest domain) decides, so
  any regex engine or none can apply it. Every =subtree= and =exact=
  row is a confirmed domain: nothing enters without being confirmed,
  by an official registry or by hand. Unconfirmed central-1 hosts stay
  in =proposed.csv=, the curation worklist. The rows with =kind=
  other than =exclude= are the short list of email domains to watch.

** Per-country data (=countries/<ISO3>_<slug>/=)

One directory per country (=FRA_france=, =USA_united_states=, …), with:

- =curated.csv= -- the confirmed domains, decided by hand
  (=domain,level,name=): =level= is =central=, =central-1= or =local=,
  =name= the body when known. A =local= row is a lower-tier label of
  a shared root (=abingdon.gov.uk= under =gov.uk=): it is out of the
  central+ scope but stays a public-sector domain. Hand-edited; the
  place to add a domain.
- =excluded.csv= -- what is out of scope altogether, as full hostnames
  (=domain,reason=): rejected proposals (not a public body, a typo…).
  Hand-edited; a domain listed here never comes back in
  =proposed.csv=, and it removes a registry's row of the same domain.
  Only present where needed.
- =proposed.csv= -- domains proposed for validation, scored 0 to 10 by
  how many sources agree, with their =level= when known (=central=,
  =central-1= or blank), HTTPS status and MX. Generated.
- =summary.md= -- a human-readable cross-check report, including the
  country's confirmed domains with their provenance. Generated.
- =sources/= -- the raw data behind the above, one folder per source,
  never edited by hand: =crtsh/<root>.csv= (the crt.sh harvest of a
  root, with probes), =probes/= (HTTPS and MX of the hosts that have
  no harvest), the official registries (=cisa/=, =lannuaire/=,
  =brreg/=, =govuk/=, each a =registered.csv= of =domain,level=),
  =wikidata/=, =directory/= and =linkgraph/=.
  =sources/linkgraph/indegree.csv= (EU + neighbours only) counts, for
  each domain, how many distinct public-sector domains of the same
  country link to it, from the crawl published by
  [[https://github.com/mgifford/eu-plus-government-scans][eu-plus-government-scans]] (=bb indegree= refreshes it); well-linked
  hosts enter =proposed.csv= with a strong score bonus.

** Master data (=data/=)

- =world-governments.csv= -- the master list of the 197 countries.
- =sources/= -- the country metadata, one table per source and one
  row per country (=country_dir= first): =cia_factbook.csv=
  (government type, capital, chief of state, head of government,
  highest courts), =un_desa.csv= (national portal, EGDI rank),
  =iana.csv= (ccTLD, manager), =oecd.csv= (membership) and
  =country_data.csv= (region, languages, population, GDP per capita).
  Generated by =bb pipeline enrich=.
- =forge-candidates.csv= -- hosts that look like software forges or
  source-code catalogs (=git.*=, =gitlab.*=, =forge.*=, =code.*=, …),
  extracted from the harvested subdomains; leads for the
  [[https://gitlab.softwareheritage.org/swh/products/insights/swh-sopc-data-sources][swh-sopc-data-sources]] catalog list.
- =known-forges.csv= -- curated list of self-hosted government forges
  (GitLab, Forgejo…), seeded from the [[https://gitlab.softwareheritage.org/swh/products/insights/swh-sopc-data-sources][swh-sopc-data-sources]] catalog
  list.
- =known-catalogs.csv= -- curated list of government source-code
  catalogs (portals pointing at code hosted elsewhere), same columns;
  kept for reference, never searched.
- =github-gov-orgs.csv= -- government GitHub organizations from
  GitHub's community-maintained [[https://government.github.com/community/][governments.yml]], plus the local
  additions of =github-gov-orgs-extra.csv= (orgs not merged upstream yet).
- =forge-unknown-swh.csv= -- forge targets (harvested + curated) that
  the [[https://archive.softwareheritage.org][Software Heritage archive]] does not know yet (checked against
  its origin-search API): candidates for archival. Each target is
  also probed over HTTPS: =forge_type= is the forge software sniffed
  from the homepage (gitlab, gitea, cgit…, or =github= for
  redirects), =http_status= the final HTTP code (=000= when
  unreachable) and =note= a short diagnostic (DNS failure, timeout,
  WAF, default page, cross-host redirect). =bb forges forges-probe=
  refreshes these columns without re-querying the SWH API. The whole
  forge/catalog channel lives in =scripts/detect-forges.clj=: it
  shares this repository's harvested data but serves the Software
  Heritage catalog goal, not the email-domain goal.
- =public-sector-domains-central+-policy.csv= -- the match policy
  described above. On mixed suffixes (=gov.uk=, =gov.br=…), =exact=
  and =exclude= rows keep subtrees below the first tier out (see
  =scripts/README.org=). Meant to be consumed directly by downstream
  pipelines, with any regex engine or none.
- A few generated mapping files linking country names to source IDs.

* Where the data comes from

- *crt.sh* -- subdomains seen in public TLS certificates (main harvest).
- *HTTPS probes* -- check that a host actually serves content.
- *Wikidata* -- ministries, parliaments, central banks, courts and their
  official websites.
- *IANA, UN/DESA, CIA World Factbook, OECD* -- other official sources
  used to cross-check candidates.
- *Official directories of public bodies* (=bb directories harvest=,
  declarative specs in =scripts/detect-from-directories.clj=) -- the
  per-country directories listed in [[https://gitlab.softwareheritage.org/swh/products/insights/swh-sopc-data-sources][swh-sopc-data-sources]]. When the directory's
  central scoping is authoritative (Norway's Enhetsregisteret,
  sector 6100) its domains are confirmed directly; when levels
  or types are mixed (Germany's Behördenwegweiser) its hosts feed
  =proposed.csv= with a strong score bonus and the =directory= source
  tag, and curation decides.

See [[https://gitlab.softwareheritage.org/swh/products/insights/swh-sopc-data-sources][swh-sopc-data-sources]] for a list of all data sources used in the
final [[https://stateofpubliccode.org/][State of Public Code]].

* Running the pipeline

Everything is driven by Babashka through =bb.edn= tasks.

#+begin_src bash
# Install Babashka (Linux / macOS)
bash <(curl -s https://raw.githubusercontent.com/babashka/babashka/master/install)

# From the repository root:
bb pipeline help     # list all sub-commands
bb pipeline all      # full run: collect + enrich + report
#+end_src

See =scripts/README.org= for the full command reference, scoring rules
and environment variables.

** Checking the classifications for your country

If you know the public administration of a country, you can help
verify that our inclusions and exclusions are right:

- =data/public-sector-domains-central+-policy.csv= -- are the
  =central= and =central-1= rows really the central government and the
  first tier below it?
- =countries/<ISO3>_<slug>/proposed.csv= and =summary.md= -- did we
  miss or misclassify domains? A confirmed domain is one line added to
  =curated.csv= (=domain,level,name=); a domain that is not a public
  body at all is one line added to =excluded.csv= (=domain,reason=).
  Never edit =proposed.csv= or =summary.md=: they are generated.
- =countries/<ISO3>_<slug>/curated.csv= -- for shared roots like
  =gov.br=: are the lower-tier labels all listed as =local=? The
  first-tier bodies (=sp.gov.br=) belong there as =central-1=.
- First-tier bodies hosted inside a =local= subtree (a state agency
  such as =educacao.sp.gov.br=) match nothing until listed
  explicitly: telling us about them is especially useful.

A merge request only needs to touch =curated.csv= or =excluded.csv=:
nothing else has to be regenerated before merging. =bb pipeline check=
tells whether the decision files are consistent (a domain both curated
and excluded, an unknown level), and the maintainer then regenerates
the consolidated lists (=bb pipeline domains=).

Please email me at =bastien.guerry@inria.fr= if you have questions and 
suggestions that don't fit in a ticket or merge request.
