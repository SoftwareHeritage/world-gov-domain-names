#+title: world-gov-domain-names -- specification

The project uses [[https://intver.org][Intentional Versioning]] (see README.org): changing a
statement of the first section increments =u= (it impacts "users") and
changing one of the second increments =i= (it impacts "integrators").
Anything this file does not mention may change in any release.

* Users

Users are the people who read the published data.

** Scope

- The countries are the UN member states and observers.
- The scope is the central government of each country and the first
  tier below it (state, Land, region, etc.). Lower tiers are out.
- Nothing promises that the lists are complete or fresh: a missing
  domain can always be added.

** Levels

| Value      | Meaning                                                       |
|------------+---------------------------------------------------------------|
| =central=    | central government, the domain and all its subdomains         |
| =central!=   | central government, the domain alone                          |
| =central-1=  | first tier below (state, Land, region, etc.), with subdomains |
| =central-1!= | first tier below, the domain alone                            |
| =local=      | lower tiers, out of scope                                     |

** Confirmed domains

- The confirmed domains of a country are those of its authoritative
  registries, plus =curated.csv=, minus =excluded.csv=.
- A curated row wins over a registry.
- =curated.csv= (=domain,level=) and =excluded.csv= (=domain,reason=) are
  edited by hand; the scripts never write them.
- A host listed in =data/public-sector-domains.csv= was seen at least
  once under a confirmed domain.

* Integrators

Integrators are the projects that read our files.

** Countries

A country is identified by its directory name (i.e. =<ISO3>_<slug>=) in
=countries/= and in the =country= column of the files below.

** =data/public-sector-domains-central+-policy.csv=

- Columns =domain,kind,country=, in that order.
- =kind= is =subtree= (the domain and everything below it), =exact= (the
  domain alone) or =exclude= (the domain and everything below it kept
  out).
- For a host, the longest =domain= that is the host or a parent of it
  decides; no match means out of scope.

** =data/public-sector-domains.csv=

- Columns =subdomain,parent_domain,country,un_status,http_status,mx=, in
  that order.
- =un_status= is =member= or =observer=.
- =http_status= is the status code of the last HTTPS probe; any value
  that is not a number is a failure; blank means never probed.
- =mx= is the MX records, =none= without MX, =dig error= on failure; blank
  means never probed.

** Per-country files

In =countries/<ISO3>_<slug>/=: =curated.csv= (=domain,level=), =excluded.csv=
(=domain,reason=) and =sources/<registry>/registered.csv= (=domain,level=).
